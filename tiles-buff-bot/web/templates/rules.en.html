🧠 Our algorithm — simply

1️⃣
How many you’re owed. One buff cuts {{ pct|round(0)|int }}% of the time you entered. We buff until {{ target_text("build") }} (construction) or {{ target_text("research") }} (research) is left. From this the site calculates the exact number of buffs and shows it: “N more owed”.
📐
Example. A 30-day research: each buff −4.5 days → 30 → 25.5 → 21 → 16.5 → 12 → 7.5. You’re owed 5 buffs; a sixth isn’t needed — it would go to waste.
2️⃣
Who’s next. {% if order == "share" %}Everyone in the queue has a bar: how much they’ve received out of what they’re owed. Whoever has the shorter bar goes next. This way we help everyone equally: small and large constructions reach their goal at roughly the same time.{% else %}The site builds the queue by the rules below.{% endif %} The first in the block (👉 next) is the one to give a buff to right now. Everyone has a note on why they’re in their spot.
🔁
Life of an entry. Join once — and you stay as long as your construction or research is running. Got a buff → pause → back in the queue. Got everything you’re owed → “🎯 reached the goal”: the entry stays, the timer runs on its own. Timer finished → the site will suggest “join with the next one”. Sped up on your own → fix the time (the site will also ask after every buff).
3️⃣
What happens when a buff is marked. The receiver’s timer drops immediately, “got” +1, “still owed” −1, and a {{ gap|int }}h pause starts. The giver’s timer drops too (in the game the buff works for both). Everyone’s queue recalculates within a few seconds.
✅
What’s guaranteed. The order is the same for everyone and visible to everyone — each player has a note on why they’re in their spot. {% if order == "share" %}Whoever has received a smaller share of what they’re owed always goes before someone with a larger share (except for “expiring”, “urgent” and the pause).{% else %}Until you get your next buff, nobody will get more than one buff more than you.{% endif %} Exactly when your buff arrives depends on how fast the alliance gives buffs; you can see it from your spot in the queue.

🤝 What I promise

  • I enter the exact time from the in-game timer. If I speed up on my own, I fix it right away (“🔧 Fix time”).
  • I give my buff in queue order — to the one marked first 👉. If I can’t find them in the game, I give it to the next one on the list.
  • Right after giving, I tap “🎁 I gave a buff” and mark who I gave it to.
  • When my construction or research is finished, I tap “🏁 Done”, so buffs don’t go to waste.
  • I don’t sign up twice and don’t enter more time than there really is.

The site can’t check the game — it all relies on honesty. Violations are visible in the buff history, and alliance leadership can remove a player from the queue.

⚙️ How buffs are distributed

✂️
One buff cuts {{ pct|round(0)|int }}% of the declared time and also works for the giver.
🎯
We buff down to a remainder of: construction — {{ target_text("build") }}, research — {{ target_text("research") }} A buff isn’t assigned if less than the lower limit would be left after it — so no buff burns to waste.
⏸
Pause after a buff — {{ gap|int }}h. Got a buff — the next one comes no sooner than {{ gap|int }}h later. During the pause, others get the buffs. This applies to everyone, “urgent” players too.

By share of what’s owed. Next is whoever has the smaller share: got 1 of 5 (20%) goes before 2 of 4 (50%). On a tie — whoever has waited longer.
