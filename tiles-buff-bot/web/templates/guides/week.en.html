🧭 The main rule

Save up and spend for events. The same speedups, shards and gear scraps score points in several events at once if you spend them in the right window. Outside events, valuable resources aren’t spent.

  • Alliance Duel (VS) — every day except Sunday, each day has its own theme.
  • Power Play (Big Game) (PP) — every day, the theme changes every 4 hours.
  • Turbo Turtle (TT) — a weekly event.

📌 Day by day — in short

  • Mon
    Gear scraps (count in both the Duel and the Turtle). Resources gathered on Sunday evening count toward Monday. Radar — Mon, Wed, Fri.
  • Tue
    Construction day. The heaviest spending of construction speedups. Trucks — A-class only, tasks — S. Since 2.6.200 the second Duel day also has pet tasks.
  • Wed
    Titan and research speedups — matches the Duel, the best return.
  • Thu
    Heroes: save recruitment cards, shards and books — spend them on Thursday 15:00–19:00. Don’t collect finished buildings in the evening — complete them on Friday.
  • Fri
    Construction and research count again. Glue for Chief gear. Saturday morning — ranking results.
  • Sat
    Army training for the Turtle, research speedups in the evening. Arcadian Conquest.
  • Sun
    No Duel: review the results, save up for the week. An hour before the day changes, check your ranking spot.

🎯 How much to score

  • In the Duel, aim for at least 5M points a day (better 10M) — for good rewards.
  • In Power Play (Big Game), fill the bar to 18 points — that’s the maximum reward.
  • Don’t chase the top every day: if you’re not making the top 50, it’s better to save resources for next week.
  • A payback example: spend 3 hours of speedups in the right window and you can get back more than 25 minutes of speedups, plus manuals, gems and Duel medals.

🧊 How this fits with alliance buffs

  • A buff cuts time immediately, on any day — no need to “save” it for an event. Give it as soon as the site tells you who to.
  • But speedups pay off more in the windows from the table. After spending them — check your time on the site (home → “Check your time” or “➕ Join → Fix time”), otherwise the queue will think you still need more buffs.
  • A long construction is best finished on construction day — completion also scores points.
