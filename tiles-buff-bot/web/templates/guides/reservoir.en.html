💧 What it is

Two alliances fight on a separate battlefield — the Canyon. Capture and hold buildings to earn purified water: for the alliance pool and for yourself. The alliance with more water when the countdown ends wins. Rewards are based on the alliance result and on personal contribution.

  • Troops don’t die. The wounded go to the field hospital: unlimited space, free healing, and everyone is healed when you leave the raid.
  • Garrison troops don’t take part: your highest-level troops are put on the battlefield automatically.
  • Bonuses from research, alliance tech, Chief talents, VIP, bonus items and skins all apply.

Based on the in-game event rules, October 2026.

🧾 Who takes part

  • 🏆
    Alliance — in the state’s top 15 by power when registration opens.
  • ⚡
    Player — Power Plant lvl 12 or higher and in the alliance before registration. Joined later — you don’t take part and get no rewards.
  • 📝
    The leader or an R4 registers. They pick up to 30 participants and 10 substitutes. Once registration ends, the roster can’t be changed.
  • ⚖️
    Opponents are matched by matchmaking rating: the stronger the Chiefs, the higher the rating.

✅ Before entering the Canyon

  • No scouting in progress.
  • Troops aren’t marching and aren’t stationed in someone else’s settlement.
  • No reinforcements at the communication station.

Your alliance is always blue on the battlefield.

Leave the raid while it’s running — and you can’t return for 10 minutes. On the upside, the wounded in the field hospital are healed when you come back.

⏱ Battle stages

  1. Stage 1: water purification facilities, water treatment plants, solar stations, helipads.
  2. Stage 2: the central reservoir, military factories and experimental testing grounds open.
  3. Stage 3: water tankers appear in random places.

💧 How to get water

  • Holding buildings is the main source. The Central Reservoir gives three times more than a plant: 1 800 a minute to the alliance pool.
  • Hitting the enemy: for every 10 000 power of defeated troops — personal water: 40 when defending a building, 80 when attacking. It doesn’t go to the alliance pool.
  • Advanced teleport is free during the event (with a cooldown). Advanced relocators work too.
  • Your settlement burns much faster than usual after a failed defense. Don’t put it out in time — it gets moved to the safe zone, and you’re out of the fight for the point.

⭐ Alliance ranks

  • A new alliance enters the ranking only after registering; its rank is set after matchmaking and recalculated every round.
  • 5 ranks. Every rank except the top one has 0 to 5 stars. 100 points = a star; after 5 stars, the next 100 points raise the rank.

🗺 Plan for the alliance

  • 1
    First — one player on each nearby plant. The first capture gives the alliance 3 000 water from each of the four plants — a quick 12 000.
  • 2
    The strongest go to the centers and the reservoir. The reservoir (stage 2) and the two centers give the most per minute: the best troops hold them.
  • 3
    Military Factory — before attacking the reservoir: +20% attack comes in handy in the assault.
  • 4
    The helipad speeds up relocators — making it easier to move forces to the point under assault.
  • 5
    Put out settlements right away, or you’ll be thrown into the safe zone.
  • 6
    Weaker participants should attack enemy troops at buildings the enemy holds: attacking gives twice as much personal water as defending.

Since troops don’t die and healing is free — fight boldly. There are no losses, and water decides it.
