🔥 Lava — the first X-SSR hero

  • Who: Stalwart faction, position 2, a versatile front-row hero — a “second tank” (bruiser): damage, burn, self-healing. Unlocks with state progress.
  • How to get: early on he isn’t in the regular recruitment card pool — don’t spend cards on him.
  • Mechanics — a chain: “Claw Combo” sets the target on fire (~3 sec, ~50% attack per sec) → “Lava Frenzy” up to +100% attack speed and extra damage to burning targets → “Lava Duality”: above 50% health — more damage, below — hits heal → “Lava Heart” raises all stats.
  • What matters in the build: attack, attack speed, healing bonus, basic attack damage, cooldown reduction and damage taken reduction — a combination, not one stat.
  • X-SSR specifics: after talent level 1 — permanent bonuses to attack and skill damage. Skills go up to level 50, the hero himself — up to 150. Needs more shards than an SSR. Part of your investment can be transferred from another hero via development exchange — check what transfers.
  • Lineup: main tank + Lava in front, three heroes behind. Example: Nikola + Lava, behind them Rosie + Tarzan + Tara. Don’t put Lava alone to take the first hit.
  • Don’t rush to drop a finished squad: a developed SSR can be more useful than an early X-SSR. Get him first, look at your shards and try him in the squad.
