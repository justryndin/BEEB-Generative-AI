🧭 What cuts construction and research time

  • 🧊
    Seasonal alliance buff — −{{ pct }}% of the declared time, for both the receiver and the giver. Once every {{ cooldown }}h, separately for construction and for research. Works on all your constructions (or research) at once. The site manages it: who to give it to is on the home page.
  • 🤝
    Alliance help (“taps”) — every ally tap cuts the timer: about 32 sec, and with a leveled-up Artifact Workshop — 45+ sec. Requires a Comms Outpost. Every day; the more active allies, the more you get.
  • ⏩
    Speedups — from events, chests, Infected Hunt. Best spent in event windows — see the “Week” guide.
  • ⚡
    24-hour base buffs — the arrow button at the top of the screen → the “Development” tab. For example, gathering speed: the max buff for 600 crystals gives +100% gathering for 24 hours — it’s recommended to always take it. Check that tab: it also shows what other speed buffs your base has available.
  • 🔬
    Research — the Development branch in the Lab: a permanent bonus to construction and research speed. See the “Lab” guide.

✅ How to account for it so the queue stays fair

The site only sees alliance buffs marked with the “Gave ✓” button. Taps, speedups and base buffs cut time every day — the site doesn’t know about them.

  1. Once a day and after every buff received, the site asks “Same in the game?” (on the home page and via notification).
  2. It matches — tap “Yes”. It doesn’t — enter the time from the timer, one button.
  3. The site recalculates how many buffs you’re still owed. If the time is already at the goal, you move to “🎯 reached the goal”, and buffs go to those who need them more.
Example. Joined with 20 days. Over two days, taps and speedups cut another 3 days, while the site thinks 18 are left. Tapped “No, the game shows something else” → entered 15 days → fewer buffs owed, and the extra buff went to someone who needs it more.

The 15% is always counted from the originally entered time — speedups don’t shrink the buff.

Since update 2.6.200, the construction and research window also shows the default time (without bonuses). On the site, enter what the timer shows right now.

💡 How to get the most out of it

  • Started a long construction — join the queue right away. While the timer is long, each buff cuts the most.
  • Give your buff as soon as it’s ready: hold it longer than {{ hold }}h — you skip your turn.
  • An alliance buff doesn’t need an “event window”: it cuts time immediately. Speedups, on the other hand, should be saved for events.
  • Turn on notifications in your profile — the site will tell you when to give and when to check your time.
