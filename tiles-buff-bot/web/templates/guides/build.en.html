🥇 What to build first

The main goal is to level up the Power Plant. It’s the main building (max level 30); overall progress and the levels of other buildings depend on it.

  1. Power Plant
  2. Lab — bonuses to everything: construction speed, hospital, training limit.
  3. Hospital — how many wounded can be saved.
  4. Garrison — how many troops the base holds.
  5. Other buildings.

Another working cycle from experienced players: Power Plant → Comms Outpost → Lab, and around again; bring the Garrison up as your army grows. Don’t level resource buildings early on — resource tiles give much more.

In our alliance: Power Plant constructions below level {{ pp_goal }} get alliance buffs with priority, and when the queue is empty — first by roulette. Set your level in your profile.

🏢 What each building is for

  • ⚡
    Power Plant — the main building. Tip: don’t open construction gifts right away — wait for a suitable event.
  • 🔬
    Lab — percentage bonuses for the whole game. Details in the “Lab” guide. A second lab lets you research in parallel.
  • 🏥
    Hospital — wounded capacity. Example: with 471K troops and a capacity of 222K, only 222K will be healed.
  • 🪖
    Garrison — max troops; at level 30 — up to 1.9M soldiers.
  • 📡
    Comms Outpost — help from allies (“taps”). Critical for playing in an alliance.
  • 🛠
    Artifact Workshop — boosts ally help: without it a tap cuts the timer by 32 sec, with it — by 45+ sec. It also cuts construction time.
  • 🎓
    Hero Academy — a steady flow of talent books.
  • 📯
    Enlistment Office — partially returns troops lost permanently.
  • 🗿
    Monuments: “Guardian of Life” and “Warbringer Monument” are worth leveling; the other minor ones (mansions, frost fortress) give small bonuses.
  • 🏆
    Golden statues — greatly boost the attack, defense and health of all heroes in squads (“Squad Power” goes up), which means arena and PvP fights. They eat a lot of resources: level them as you can, a noticeable gain starts around level 10–15.
  • 🏠
    Houses — up to level 10. Kitchen — tomato pizza increases satiety.
  • 👷
    Second builder — two constructions at once. One of the most useful investments for minimal spenders.

Any building can be moved: press and hold on it.
