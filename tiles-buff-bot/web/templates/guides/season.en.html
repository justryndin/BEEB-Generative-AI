❄️ What it is

“Era of Blizzard” is the fourth season. It does not start for everyone at once, but according to season progress in your state. New monsters, locations, companions and mechanics.

🧊 Main mechanics

  • 🔥
    Doomsday Furnace — the season’s central building. You upgrade it to raise Cold Resistance and keep your settlement from freezing.
  • 🏯
    Bastions and Cities — alliances capture them on the map. In Bastion Clash, the event page shows the time (UTC) when each alliance took its highest bastion. In City Contest, since 2.6.100, monsters in level 4–6 cities have become weaker.
  • ⛽
    Natural Gas — a new resource on the map, needed to capture bastions and cities.
  • 🧌
    Frost Giant — a special creature you can raise.
  • 🛡
    Super-alloy gear — alloy gear at max level and stars can be upgraded to super-alloy: higher level and stars, extra bonuses.

🏭 Seasonal class: Coal League or Frost Court

In the season everyone picks a class — each has 5 active skills. The choice costs a special item (1 pc.).

🔵 Coal League — development

  • Construction — +15% construction speed for you and a chosen ally for 60 min.
  • Joint — +15% research speed for you and a chosen ally for 60 min.
  • Quick — instantly −4 h of construction on all buildings.
  • Sparks and Silver — instantly 4 000 coal and foil.
  • Ruthless — −5% damage taken for 60 min.

🟠 Frost Court — war

  • Fierce Attack — +5% damage for your troops for 1 h.
  • United — +5% damage for all rally participants for 1 h (if several such skills are active, only the highest one works).
  • Counterattack — +5% damage when defending your settlement for 1 h.
  • First Aid — instantly heal 20 000 wounded in the hospital (not those already being healed).
  • Mighty Strike — instantly get 10 000 high-tier troops.
The buffs this site hands out are the Coal League skills “Construction” and “Joint”. Only players who picked Coal League can give them. Builders and heavy researchers — Coal League; the alliance’s strike force — Frost Court.

📈 Season progress points

Since version 2.6.0 there are three new sources of points:

  1. Chilling Rescue — rescuing animals trapped in ice.
  2. Doomsday Express — deliveries.
  3. Alliance Ice Clearing — helping allies clear ice. Help each other: it pays off for both you and the alliance.

If your state got a bye in State Contest matchmaking, the weekly seasonal task for killing troops counts automatically.

📌 Good to know

  • After leaving an alliance you have to wait 48 hours before taking part in Clash of Frost and Fire, Clash of Light and Night and Dragon Isle Clash (since 2.6.100).
  • In Trade Contest, you can only scout and attack trading posts within your season group.
  • The season monument is no longer in the settlement but in the Hall of Glory.
  • “Game House” in the event task list: mini-games and a token exchange shop.
  • Before spending rare resources and speedups, check how much time is left until the seasonal event ends.
