🎯 How to win

The event runs every Saturday for 3 hours. The winner is the alliance that holds Arcadia for a total of 1.5 hours — or longest over the whole battle. It appoints the Governor.

🗼 Towers

Four towers — north, south, west and east. While an alliance holds a tower, the Arcadia garrison loses 2% of its troops per strike, and strikes get more frequent — from once every 4 minutes to once a minute. A tower also gives points, just like Arcadia.

🚚 Relocation

  • 1 hour before the start, settlements in the alert zone are forcibly and randomly relocated — wait 10–15 minutes before moving.
  • From 50 minutes before the start until the event ends, relocation inside the alert zone is free, but shields (Peace Flare) don’t work — any switched on earlier will be removed.
  • Pick a spot close to Arcadia or a tower in advance — shorter march, more attacks.

🏆 Points and rewards

  • Your own losses — base points (higher for high-tier troops). Enemy kills — more points.
  • Holding Arcadia or a tower — 10,000 points per minute.
  • The Governor gets skills (fast gathering, golden eggs, fast search), hands out honorary and penalty titles and Governor packs (max one per player per event), plus special skins.
  • State posts: strategist (+10% speed), “Mighty Bull” (+80% production), “Patriot” (+50% training).

⚔️ Tactics

If we’re fighting for Governor:

  • The strongest players quickly knock out the defenders and take Arcadia, the rest reinforce the garrison.
  • Keep attack and defense balanced — you need people online and defenders without “gaps”.
  • Shifts: split the alliance into 3–4 groups — attack, defense, tower defense.
  • Joint rallies at the same time: up to 5 ally teams in each rally.
  • If you can’t break through head-on — take the towers: they knock 2% off the garrison per strike.

If we’re fighting for points:

  • Attack nonstop — both losses and kills give points.
  • Lower-tier troops for regular fights, strong ones for precise strikes.
  • Towers are easier to hold and give the same points — 10,000 per minute. Great for growing alliances.
  • Catch the moments when ownership changes or players go offline.

Prep: A-class trucks, S tasks. It’s handy to mark “I’ll be there / Can’t make it” in alliance events.
