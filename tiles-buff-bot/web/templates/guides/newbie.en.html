🎮 The game in a nutshell

Tiles Survive is about surviving and growing a shelter: you explore tiles and expand your territory, build and upgrade buildings, hook up electricity, assign survivors to roles (hunters, cooks, lumberjacks), gather resources in different biomes, recruit heroes and team up in alliances against common threats.

🤝 The key is a strong alliance

Success in the game depends heavily on your alliance: more active players means more help “taps”, rallies and info. Help your allies — it makes things easier for them, and so for you too.

🧭 First month as a free player — step by step

  1. Gems go to VIP. VIP gives bonuses to everything: production, march speed, training, hero attack. The goal is VIP 5 early on and VIP 8 within the first month (VIP 8 unlocks 5 march slots).
  2. The Power Plant is the main goal. Details in “Base development”.
  3. Valuable resources — only for events. Spend speedups, shards and scraps in the right windows — see “Week”.
  4. Heroes: focus on 2–3. Don’t level everyone in a row, save universal shards.
  5. Titan — free materials: Reservoir Raid, Doomsday Express (B and A trucks), boss attack (5 attempts a day). The goal is level 100 and up.
  6. Map lands — buildings are free for clearing the map; level the key ones to 15.

💸 If you do spend a little

  • Second builder — two constructions at once.
  • Second lab — two research projects at once.
  • Gems: VIP first (at least to 8), then stamina — for zombie hunting (speedups, blueprints, chests).

🔁 Daily routine

  • Infected Hunt, Alpha Beast once a day, alliance rallies (turn on auto-join).
  • Square radar missions — help an ally (they collect the reward without spending stamina).
  • Alliance Duel — the day’s tasks.
  • Arena — 5 attempts 10 minutes before the day changes.
  • Alliance buffs: open the site → give to the player shown on the home page → “Gave ✓”.

🛡 Gear and Chief

  • Quality: white → blue → purple → gold. In Turbo Turtle, 20 medals = a gold armor piece.
  • Chief gear shows up after about a month: crafting — 10 glue (from rallies), upgrading — glue and blueprints (Recycling Center: 1200 coins = 1 blueprint).
  • For attack — dagger, greaves, radio first. Pick one item and level it to 15.
