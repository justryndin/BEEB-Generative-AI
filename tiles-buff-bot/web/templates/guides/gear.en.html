🧭 The essentials

When a hero’s gear reaches the required number of stars, a special stats slot unlocks — and it can be reforged. First decide what role the hero has: tank, damage dealer or support; their faction (Stalwart, Aeronaut, Mariner, Rover) and troop type (Guard, Gunner, Marksman). A stat that fits the role works much better.

Reforged stats apply in all fights — both in marches and in map exploration.

📋 General stats (helmet, armor, greaves)

  • Damage bonus — the best pick for damage dealers. Damage reduction — the best pick for survivability. These are the top legendary stats.
  • Healing bonus; damage and defense vs. basic attacks; damage and defense vs. active skills.

🎯 For a specific slot

  • Helmet — attack: hero attack, attack of the matching troop type, faction hero attack. Special — crit bonus (very good for Rover heroes with crit skills).
  • Armor — defense: hero, troop, faction defense. Special — −15% active skill cooldown, stacks with several of the same. For heroes who live off their skills.
  • Greaves — health: hero, troop, faction health. Special — all stats for allied heroes in battle: support for the whole team.

“Troop” and “faction” stats only work if they match the hero.

🧩 What to pick

  • 🛡
    Tank: damage reduction, defense on armor, health on greaves, defense vs. active skills.
  • ⚔️
    Damage dealer: damage bonus, attack on helmet, active skill damage or crit bonus.
  • 💚
    Support: healing bonus plus survival stats.

Golden rule: a match beats rarity. A stat that fits the hero’s faction or troops can be more useful than an “off-fit” legendary one.
