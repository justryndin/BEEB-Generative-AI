🧭 The main rule: event first, then the button

Almost any action — construction, research, hero upgrades, gathering — can be done “just because”, or in the right phase so you also earn event points. Before spending anything, open the events and check the timers and conditions.

The schedule by day of the week and the windows for speedups are in the “Week” guide.

🎲 Power Play (Big Game)

Every day, six themes of 4 hours each: heroes, troops, research, gear, Behemoth, Chief Collection. The windows start at 03, 07, 11, 15, 19 and 23 MSK (00, 04, 08, 12, 16, 20 UTC). There is currently no construction theme in the calendar.

  • Spend a theme’s resources in its window: shards and books — in “heroes”, cells and serums — in “Behemoth”, blueprints and parts — in “Chief Collection”.
  • A research is almost done, but the “Tech Research” window comes later — don’t complete it right away.
  • Keep speedups for the window where they’ll unlock chests.
  • Resource chests in your backpack are a flexible reserve. Open them for no reason — and the flexibility is gone.

The full weekly calendar and overlaps with the Duel are in the “Week” guide; what’s running right now — on the home page in the “VS today” card.

⛏ Underground Goldmine

You occupy mines, extract gold and risk running into rivals. The higher the mine level, the better the rewards and the higher the chance of being kicked out.

  • Don’t send a weak squad to a contested spot and don’t forget about it — the mine needs to be watched.
  • Before marching, check the mine level, extraction time, neighbors and the shop. A modest but quiet spot is often better than a top one for a couple of minutes.
  • Don’t trade the shop currency for trifles on the first day — save up for one important thing (e.g. Precise Blueprints for the collection).

🆕 Since 2.6.0 you enter the mine via the task list in the event, not via the event center. Since 2.6.200 the squad status and timer are shown there too.

⚔️ Alliance Duel (VS)

What matters is the alliance’s total contribution. Each day has its own theme: construction, research, heroes, gathering, training, battles.

  • Don’t spend ahead of time. Tomorrow is hero day — hold on to books and shards. Construction day is coming — don’t collect a big building.
  • For officers — short commands: “no hero upgrades until the evening”, “don’t spend speedups”, “marches on gathering”.

🆕 2.6.0: in the Duel League — alliances are matched by power, stars for wins, rank promotion based on the number of stars by the end of the league. Pilot: during a Duel raid, defenders in the settlement take no losses (attackers — as usual). 2.6.200: on the second Duel day — pet tasks (where pets are unlocked).

🏁 Stage race (ranks Bronze → Peak)

An event of 5–8 stages, 1–2 days each. Every stage has its own task list and its own points; the sum of all stages is the race result. Milestones give rewards, and placings over the distances give race medals for the event shop.

  • 6 ranks: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Legend, Peak. The higher the rank, the better the milestone rewards.
  • Rank changes at the end of each stage. Up: the group’s top 4 in Bronze, top 3 in Silver, Gold, Diamond and Legend. Down: the bottom 3 in Silver, the bottom 4 in Gold, Diamond, Legend and Peak — and everyone who scored no points.
  • You start the next race at the rank you reached; newcomers start at the lowest. So score at least a few points on every stage: zero means a guaranteed drop.
  • Players inactive for a long time are skipped in opponent matching, but stay in the stage ranking and get milestone rewards.

Stages this week (per the in-game rules, October 2026):

  1. Hero gear upgrade and enhancement, reforge hammers and reforge tape, research speedups, troop training and upgrades.
  2. Construction speedups, pet marks (legendary, epic, rare), pet upgrades, gear and reforging.
  3. Behemoth cells and serums, biogenic protein (except exclusive), research speedups.
  4. Hero coupons, advanced and standard recruitment tokens, hero shards, skill manuals.
  5. Common parts and Precise Blueprints (Chief Collection), gear upgrade and enhancement, reforging.
  6. Behemoth, hero shards, manuals, construction speedups and research speedups.
  7. Everything at once: 25 kinds of tasks, including gathering and defeating the infected and the unholy.

Diamond packs also give points on every stage. Combine stages with Power Play windows and Duel days — one action counts in several events at once.

🦁 Lion Chase

An alliance boss. Only R4 and above can start it or schedule its time. Raids on the hunting ground don’t cost stamina. Rewards at the end are based on damage dealt.

  • The reward multiplier for the whole alliance depends on the damage of the top player: up to 299.99M — ×1, 300M–399.99M — ×2, 400M–599.99M — ×3, from 600M — ×4. The strongest player should hit for the max.
  • When the alliance’s total damage reaches milestones, all participants get extra rewards.
  • Beast Traps are donated to the hunting ground: it levels up and gives warrior attack — lvl 2: +40%, lvl 3: +60%, lvl 4: +80%, lvl 5: +100%. The level resets at the end of the event; contributions above the max don’t carry over to next time. The boost only works in the current event and is applied again on every new attempt.
  • Can’t be started within 24 h before a season starts. After joining a new alliance — 48 h without taking part.

When it can be started again is shown by the cooldown timer on the event screen. Turn on “Auto-join” if you often miss the start.

💧 Reservoir Raid

Alliance vs alliance in the Canyon for purified water: troops don’t die, healing is free. Up to 30 players + 10 substitutes take part, Power Plant lvl 12+. Details in the separate “Reservoir Raid” guide.

🧗 Glory Expedition

Progress through stages — it rewards consistency, not a last-day sprint.

  • Read the stage conditions: defeat enemies, spend items, build up activity… Otherwise you’ll spend what you need too early.
  • Close the available tasks every day and don’t skip free progress.
  • When choosing rewards, take what solves your problem right now — rare isn’t always more useful.

🏟 Arena, 3×3 Arena and Infected Siege

  • 1️⃣
    The Arena tests your main lineup. Look at the fight, not the power: who falls first, is there enough healing, does the defense get broken. If you lose to equals — the problem is most likely the hero combination.
  • 3️⃣
    The 3×3 Arena tests the depth of your account. Two working teams are better than one pretty one and two “made of leftovers”. 🆕 Since 2.6.0 you enter via the “Exploration” menu, not the task list.
  • 🛡
    The Infected Siege is an exam for the whole alliance: defensive lineups, hospital, healing, presets, being online. Prepare in advance, not when the army is already in the hospital.

🐢 Turbo Turtle

  • Figure out which actions move progress, and pick tasks with the best ratio of points to cost.
  • If gathering counts — send marches so they come back in the right window.
  • You don’t have to fight for the top: close the main rewards and stop — the reserve will come in handy in the Duel.
  • Keep some marches free if the alliance is preparing an activity.

👹 Terror Extermination

A recurring PvE: you hit a strong target and clear damage stages. The goal is to squeeze the most out of every attempt.

  1. Check your lineup: who deals damage, who buffs, who tanks.
  2. Do a test fight and look at the report.
  3. Swap one hero and compare the damage. A noticeable difference — you’re on the right track; none — don’t spend resources blindly.

For a newcomer, steady stage rewards are enough — the top of the ranking often doesn’t pay off.

🎡 Seasonal and special events

  • 💼
    Future Tycoon — progress on the bar every day, not everything on the last evening. First figure out what moves the bar. In the shop, pick your main prize in advance and save up for it — Precise Blueprints show up here.
  • 🗝
    Chamber of Secrets — read the rules. Find the key prizes on the bar and decide which milestone you need. Take the main rewards, beyond that compare cost and benefit.
  • 🎡
    Lucky Wheel — check the prize list and the reward bar for the number of spins. Free spins — always; paid ones — only if you have enough to reach the milestone you need.
  • 🎈
    Balloon Festival — simple, no power needed, just consistency: free attempts every day. Spend the currency before the event ends, and in the shop compare what’s more useful right now.
  • 🍀
    Leprechaun Pinball — lots of luck involved, set yourself a limit. Take the free attempts, check the bar and the shop. It’s an extra, not the backbone of your progress.

🧩 Small events — don’t skip them

  • Hero Raid — hero shards and materials. First check whether your lineup needs the hero.
  • Special Gear — invest in those who actually go into battle, not in everyone. See “Hero gear reforging”.
  • Doomsday — deliveries, trucks, attempts. Look at the task quality, time and prize. (Probably the Doomsday Express.)
  • Season Supplies — help keep up the pace in seasonal activities.

🆕 Doomsday Express, 2.6.0: in states that have gone through at least one season, trucks give random boxes of legendary hero shards (heroes you already have) instead of Ray’s shards. 2.6.200: status and timer — in the event’s task list.

❄️ Big seasonal events

A separate layer of the game: a new map, seasonal buildings and resources, enemies, weekly progress, shops, passes and big alliance goals. You can’t fall behind from the first days — you slow down both yourself and the alliance.

  • Everyone: daily tasks, chat, help with markers, clearing, no random marches.
  • R4/R5: explain the plan — what we capture, what we save up, where the markers are, when the battles are. It’s not the loudest alliance that wins, but the most organized one.

🆕 Since 2.6.100: after leaving an alliance you have to wait 48 hours before taking part in Dragon Isle Clash, Clash of Light and Night and Clash of Frost and Fire. More in the “Season” guide.

🥇 How to set priorities

  1. Account and alliance growth: Alliance Duel, big seasons, arenas, Underground Goldmine, combat events.
  2. Good shops and rare items.
  3. Mini-events — calmly take the free attempts.
Three questions for every day: what ends first? which rewards are the most useful? which actions can be combined? If one action closes two events — do it first.

Spend real money only for a specific goal and a specific mode, not “just in case”.
