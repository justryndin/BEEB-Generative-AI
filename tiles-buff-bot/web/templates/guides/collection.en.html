🧭 The key points

The Chief Collection is six items that boost your whole account, not just one hero like regular gear. A single level gives a little, but six items × several qualities add up to a very noticeable boost.

The trap is going only by power. A big jump in power doesn’t mean the upgrade is the best one: check which stat (attack, defense, health) your main lineup actually needs.

🆕 Update 2.6.100 (September 17, 2026): the collection got a major rework — a shared screen with all its bonuses, set bonuses (collect items of the same quality — get an extra bonus) and new mythic sets. The DTF guide came out before this patch, so the numbers below may have partly changed — check against the game.

🎯 What to level first — based on your lineup

The old “universal” order watch → knife → lantern → diary → binoculars → compass relied on the items giving different power. Now their power is almost even, so following it blindly no longer makes sense. Pick the stat you need first — then the item that gives it.

Lineup corePriorityWhy
StrongmenAttack → Defense → HealthThey already have plenty of survivability, attack raises damage more noticeably. Defense above health: Tara’s and Nikola’s shields depend on it.
Rogues, aggressiveAttack → Health → DefenseFor the arena, Reservoir Raid, the Oil Battle — take the enemy apart before they respond. Candy and Layla make good use of HP. The downside — they’re worse at holding a point.
Rogues, reliableAttack → Defense → HealthA bit less damage, but more stable in long fights and on defense.

If the same lineup both attacks and holds positions — go with the balanced option.

🪜 All at once or one item to the max?

It depends on quality: up to blue it pays to level evenly, from blue on — finish them one at a time.

  1. Gray — all six to level 10, then upgrade to green.
  2. Green — all evenly to level 15. The first levels are cheap and give a lot: from level 1 to 10 a green item grows from about 130K to 799K power, and the step up to blue adds about 390K more.
  3. Blue — time to rush. A level costs 30 Precise Blueprints, and the step up to purple costs 150 (like five levels). Pick an item for your build → to level 15 → straight to purple → the next one.
  4. Purple — same scheme, but to level 20 → straight to gold → the next one.
  5. Gold (legendary) — there’s no further quality upgrade, just level up to 30.
In short: gray to 10 → all green to 15 → blue one by one to 15 and into purple → purple one by one to 20 and into gold → gold to 30.

Don’t spread blue items out “+2 each”: from this stage it pays more to focus and complete the step up. Note: since 2.6.100 there are bonuses for a set of one quality — sometimes it may be better to bring the whole set up to one color. There are no exact calculations for the new system yet.

💸 How much it costs

  • One item to legendary quality (without leveling up the legendary levels): roughly 65,190 parts + 1,840 Precise Blueprints.
  • The whole collection of six: about 391,140 parts + 11,040 blueprints.
  • You need more parts, but they’re easier to get. The main shortage in the late game is Precise Blueprints.

Why a quality upgrade is so valuable: the matching combat bonus at the first level of the new quality jumps from 1.5% (uncommon) to 12.5% (rare), then to 24.5% (epic), and legendary starts at 40%. Sometimes it’s better not to squeeze out the last levels but to get the item ready for the next rarity sooner.

📦 Where to get parts and Precise Blueprints

  • ⚙️
    Parts — scarce at first, later they come in noticeably more often. The main source is Distorted Alphas: hit the highest ones you can handle. Don’t rush to buy more at the first shortage — first count how many you really need to reach your goal.
  • 🧟
    Wandering Infected — the first win of the day is guaranteed to give 3 blueprints. Over a month that’s about 90 almost for free.
  • ♻️
    Recycling Center — spare hero shards → currency → blueprints in the shop. It still works after the Wall of Honor appears. Just make sure the shards really are spare.
  • ⛏
    Underground Goldmine — its shop sometimes has blueprints; if they’re in the rotation — make them a priority, on par with valuable shards. Since 2.6.0 you enter it via the task list in the event.
  • 🏰
    Arcadia (for clearing and achievements) and Kingdom vs Kingdom (KvK) — also give blueprints. Don’t mindlessly spend Reservoir Raid currency on blueprints: there are more valuable things there.

🎪 Events — the main supply

  • Future Tycoon — one of the best sources: blueprints in the event shop. Don’t waste the currency on minor stuff.
  • Partner Paradise — blueprints in chests and stage rewards; spend attempts on the milestone rewards you need.
  • Chamber of Secrets — look at the floor rewards first; save up attempts and clear several floors at once if blueprints are close.
  • Anniversary, holidays and other limited-time events — look for blueprints in the shop first, not cosmetics.

Hold off on big collection upgrades if there’s an event coming where they give points or rewards. Spend real money only for a specific goal: count how much you’re short of the quality upgrade.
