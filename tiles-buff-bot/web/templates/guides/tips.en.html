  • 1
    Save — then spend. Sync your spending with events: Duel (every day except Sun), Turbo Turtle (weekly), Power Play (Big Game) (tasks every 4h). Finish long constructions in a Power Play window. The Power Play bar at 18 points is the max reward.
  • 2
    Don’t build extras. Power Plant → Comms Outpost → Lab, around again. Garrison — as your army grows.
  • 3
    Weekly plan — in the “Week” guide.
  • 4
    Your army is a resource. It’s meant to be spent: Arcadia battles (a top-100 spot), Duels (gems for first place), and then train a new one — points in the Turtle.
  • 5
    Gems: VIP first (at least 8), then stamina — for zombie hunting.
  • 6
    Radar. Square missions (help an ally) first. Save round ones for Mon, Wed, Fri. Stamina reduction tech — up to level 21.
  • 7
    Heroes. For economy — Eva (gathering). For combat — Rosie (+10% attack for all), Becky (−10% enemy defense), Nikola (tank). Watch the lineup bonuses: e.g. +15% health for 4 heroes of the same kind.
  • 8
    Tech: economy (construction, research and training speed, “+5000 troops”), gathering, zombies (speedups), intel, duel (chests 7–9). Details in “Lab”.
  • 9
    Coal. Around day 55 you’ll start running short on coal — look out for “abandoned” bases in advance; you can take up to 70% of what they’ve stored.
  • 10
    Rallies and zombies. Around the bases of top alliances there are lots of strong rallies — drop the coordinates in chat. Hit max-level zombies, join other people’s rallies, don’t waste attempts on weak ones.
  • 11
    Alpha zombies — huge resources and chests for the whole alliance (up to 8 hours of speedups for 100 people). Very worthwhile for the alliance.
  • 12
    Aggressive economy: gather resources → train an army → spend it in battle → train again.
  • 13
    Safety: before bed, untick troop types on the barricades — you won’t lose anything if you get burned.
  • 14
    Small things: Doomsday Express — in the second half of the day; start resource gathering on Saturday evening; in the VIP shop, stamina beats speedups; on Sunday, an hour before the day changes, check your ranking spot.
