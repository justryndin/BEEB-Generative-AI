🧭 The essentials

The Lab gives bonuses to everything: construction and research speed, heroes, resources, troops. A fully researched branch can give +100–200% to its stat.

A scheme that works well: in the first month, take the main branches to about 60% and move on to the next one; in the second month, finish them to 100%.

🥇 Branch order

  1. Development — to 60%. Construction and research speed, training and healing, hospital capacity, training limit. It speeds up everything else — that’s what you need first.
  2. Economy — to 60%. Resource production and gathering speed. The “+5000 troops per squad” bonus is especially valuable.
  3. Heroes — to 60%. Attack, defense, health of all heroes.
  4. Warfare — to 40%. Takes long, weaker effect — have a base, but it can wait.
  5. Intel Post — to 100% as soon as it opens (after Lab level 24). Intel mission quality and lots of power.
  6. Alliance Duel — to 100% by the second month. Unlocks chests 4–9 in the Duel (hero shards, titan cells, speedups, medals, scraps). Researched with coins from those same chests.
  7. Infected Conquest — 60–80%. Damage and defense against monsters, speedup items. Elite infected bonuses go only to the rally leader — take turns leading.
  8. Special Forces — late game: the only branch that unlocks T10 troops. Needs lots of medals — close out the Duel chests first.

🌳 All branches — in short

  • Development — construction and research speed, training
  • Economy — resource production and storage
  • Heroes — hero attack, defense and health
  • Troops — troop stats and load capacity
  • Intel Post — intel missions, coal and resources
  • Alliance Duel — chests 4–9 and point multipliers (for medals)
  • Special Forces — T10 troops, march capacity (medals + resources)
  • Infected Hunt — speedups, XP and chests from the map
  • Doomsday Express — trucks, cells and behemoth XP
  • Might of the Ocean — bonuses for sailor-faction heroes

💡 Tips

  • Keep the Power Plant ahead of the Lab — otherwise the Lab level will hit a ceiling.
  • A second lab — parallel research, one of the best investments.
  • Spend research speedups in the windows from the “Week” guide (Wednesday, Saturday evening) — you’ll get both the bonus and event points.
  • Long research — join the buff queue: while the timer is long, each buff saves the most. After using speedups, check your time on the site.
